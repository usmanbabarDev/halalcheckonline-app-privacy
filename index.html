Privacy Policy

Halal Check Online app for Android · Last updated 15 October 2026

Halal Check Online is a free app that helps you check whether food is halal, with prayer times, a Qibla compass and more. It has no accounts, and we do not run servers that receive your data.

What stays on your phone

Your settings (school of thought, strictness, theme and text size), favourites, scan history, chosen location, prayer-notification choices and tasbih counts are stored only on your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them. You can also clear your history and forget your location in the app's Settings.

Camera

The camera is used only when you scan a barcode or photograph an ingredients label. Barcodes and label text are read on your phone by Google ML Kit. Photos are not uploaded or kept.

Location

Your location is used on your phone to calculate prayer times, the Qibla direction and the nearest mosques, and only after you tap "Use my location". You can choose a town from a list instead. Your location is never sent to us.

Notifications

Prayer-time (adhan) notifications are scheduled on your phone and are off until you turn them on.

Information sent to other services

  • Barcode lookups: if a scanned product is not saved in the app, its barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts to fetch the ingredients.
  • Live Makkah and Madinah: these open the official broadcasts on YouTube, which applies its own privacy policy.
  • Advertising: the app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address and information about ad interactions, to show and measure ads and prevent fraud, as described in how Google uses information from apps that use its services. In the UK and EU you are asked for consent first, and you can change it at any time in the app under Settings › Privacy choices for ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings.

Children

The app is not designed for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact
